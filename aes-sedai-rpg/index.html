    Weaving

    Single threads are raw flows. Two or three threads form woven weaves such as Fireball (Fire + Air), Air Shield (Air + Spirit) or Healing Flow (Water + Spirit). You must be taught a weave before you can form it; unknown patterns fizzle and strain you.

    Over-channeling

    Every weave adds strain. Weaving above your rank, below a weave's strength, or with an empty reserve multiplies it. Strained slows recovery, Volatile risks backlash, Critical risks sudden burnout, and the top of the meter burns you out: a permanent loss of Strength and Reserve. Three burnouts and you are stilled.

    The Tower

    Failing a mission costs prestige, may scar you permanently, and earns a strike. Three strikes and you are put out of the Tower. Advanced weaves stay sealed until you swear the Three Oaths. After that, the Oaths forbid harming humans unless your life is in danger: bind and seize them instead.
